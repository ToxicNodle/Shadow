Common questions

Where does the data actually come from?

EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program (every US facility >25k tCO2e/yr — public), USAspending.gov federal contract recipients (public), and named industrial buildings from OpenStreetMap. All public-domain US government / OSM data. Re-pulled annually so you're working with fresh records.

Is contact info included?

Company name + city + state + NAICS code are always included. Direct contact info (phone, email, decision-maker) is NOT in the base pack — most installers run their own enrichment via Apollo, Clearbit, or ZoomInfo. We give you the high-fit accounts; your existing tools find the human.

How is this different from ZoomInfo / Apollo / Lead411?

Those tools sell you contacts. We sell you solar-qualified accounts. Every row has a 0-100 fit score, recommended ownership model (direct buy vs PPA), estimated system size, and an industry-specific sales script. ZoomInfo doesn't know your customer base needs a tax-exempt-aware PPA pitch; we do.

Are these shared leads?

No. Exclusive by default. Unlike lead vendors that sell the same prospect to 5-10 installers, each territory pack is sold to a single buyer. Plus, every row has a public-domain provenance URL linking to the EPA, USAspending.gov, or OSM source record — you can verify the data yourself. No black-box scraping, no fabricated contacts.

What's the provenance URL?

Every lead in your CSV includes a direct link to the original government source record. EPA facility? Link to ghgdata.epa.gov. Federal awardee? Link to usaspending.gov. This is an anti-fraud signal no competitor can fake — the underlying data is authoritative US government or OpenStreetMap.

What if I don't close a deal from the leads?

30-day money-back if you don't get at least one qualified conversation. Email support@helioscout.io with your outreach record — we'll refund and keep the relationship intact.

Can I resell these?

The data is public domain — yes, technically. But a lot of the value is in our enrichment (fit scores, sales scripts, NPV math), which is licensed for your own use. National Pack buyers get a sublicense addendum on request.
